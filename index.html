Built to give back

One Gunner Executive Car Wash was started by Levyson Phiri, a police officer at Lilayi Paramilitary. He serves his community in uniform, and he wanted to give back to it in another way: by building a place where people in Lilayi and across Lusaka can have their vehicles cared for properly.

Our promise is simple: honest work, a clean finish and a warm welcome, every day of the week.
